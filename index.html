Jungle Rampage Pinball

Jungle Rampage Pinball is a free jungle-temple pinball game you play in the browser. Plunge for a skill shot, light the ramps and the top lanes, spell JUNGLE, and lock the tiger for multiball on the way to the Temple of Gold. Nothing to install: open this page and the table launches as soon as the art and playfield are ready.
